# Design System: Yönetimcell

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Warm architectural clarity."**

The implemented identity pairs the warmth of an architectural publication with the precision of a financial workspace. Parchment surfaces, copper actions, charcoal identity bands and original courtyard/shared-space photography make property management feel grounded in the places people live. Dark mode translates the same roles into charcoal surfaces and apricot actions.

Marketing uses compact editorial sections, a Manrope homepage headline with a copper true-italic serif companion, and actual populated product evidence. Operational screens keep compact Manrope typography, readable labels and comparable figures. Shared color roles connect both surfaces without imposing a landing-page composition on the panel. Brand strings remain centralized in `packages/config/src/site.ts`; Yönetimcell is the currently configured placeholder, and Vekil is not a shipped rebrand.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm, restrained surfaces with hairline divisions.
- Editorial marketing headings; practical sans forms and tables.
- Semantic light/dark themes and meaningful financial status colors.
- Real product captures with disclosed image retouch and sample-data captions, interactive illustrations with example labels, and traceable architectural imagery.

### Primary

Copper (`primary`) carries actions, links and progress in light mode; apricot (`primary-dark`) carries the same roles in dark mode. `primary-fg` supplies the theme-appropriate text color. The identity role `ink` is separate: logo marks, footer and dark bands use it rather than assuming every brand surface is an action.

### Secondary

Apricot `secondary` and `accent` provide supporting emphasis, each with its own foreground, hover and soft-surface roles. Use the semantic foreground paired with its background rather than assuming light text fits an accent.

### Neutral

`canvas` is parchment in light mode and charcoal in dark mode. `surface`, `surface-raised` and `surface-sunken` establish quiet hierarchy; light raised/sunken roles currently share values with surface/canvas, while dark mode differentiates them. `fg`, `fg-muted` and `fg-subtle` organize text. `border` separates content; `border-strong` defines controls. `ring`, `hover` and `selected` communicate interaction.

### Status and data

Success, warning and danger retain green, amber and red financial meanings. Informational notices use a warm copper/apricot family. Use each status's soft, foreground and border roles together; never recolor overdue debt as a brand accent.

The six `chart-*` slots, seven `seq-*` steps and `div-neg/mid/pos` are independent data palettes. Blue remains legitimate in charts and numeric ramps. Assign categorical slots consistently by series, preserve the existing six-slot ordering, and group additional series as “Diğer.” Keep the chart legend and table alternative: color is supplementary information. Dark ramps and categorical values are explicitly mapped, not automatically inverted.

**The Semantic Role Rule.** Component JSX uses `bg-canvas`, `text-fg`, `border-border`, `bg-primary` and related roles. Hex values and raw Tailwind color utilities belong outside component JSX; do not add `dark:` color pairs. Theme mapping remains centralized under `data-theme`.
